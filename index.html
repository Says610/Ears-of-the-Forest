THE BEGINNING
"This school trip is going to be awesome!" you said to your friend.
You both were excited to explore the ancient forest...

The sounds of the forest are changing...
Find your way out before it's too late.

You made it out alive!

You and your friend celebrate at home,
grateful to have survived the nightmare forest.

Slowly, you became one with the forest...
Now you watch over it, forever.
