How to Play

Movement

• WASD - Move around the forest

• SPACE - Jump over obstacles

• SHIFT - Sprint (drains stamina)

• MOUSE - Look around

Equipment

• F - Toggle flashlight (battery drains)

• H - Use medkit to heal

• B - Replace flashlight battery

• ESC - Pause game / Open menu

Survival Tips

• Listen for wolf howls - they hunt in packs

• Stay in light to reduce fear

• Avoid caves unless necessary

• Collect resources to craft items

• Watch your stamina - running out leaves you vulnerable

Dangers

• Wolves will stalk, circle, then attack

• Darkness increases fear (affects sanity)

• The cave holds a powerful boss wolf

• Time passes - events trigger at 3, 5, and 10 minutes
